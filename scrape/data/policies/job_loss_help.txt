TOPIC: Job Loss Help
QUESTION: What to do if you are unable to make your loan payments due to job loss?
--- POLICY ANSWER ---
If you lose your job, log in to your account and activate the B-Secure service immediately to modify your payment schedule. You can apply for a principal payment holiday. Interest continues to accrue during a payment holiday, and your loan maturity date will extend. Provide unemployment registration documents if requested by support.
