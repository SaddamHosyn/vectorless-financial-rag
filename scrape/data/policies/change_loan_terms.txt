TOPIC: Change Loan Terms

QUESTION: How can I change the loan terms?

--- POLICY ANSWER ---

You can find this functionality in your self-service account under the section “Change loan schedule”. The changes will be applied without any additional cost.
What are the rescheduling possibilities?
Choose your payment date (1.-27 of each month.)
Change your loan duration (1–120 months. Period can be changed to 3, 6, 12, 24, 48, 60 or 120 months.)
Take a payment holiday (you will pay only interest and commission during the period of 3, 6 or 12 months.)
Confirm payment schedule changes
Once you've made the changes in your payment schedule, follow these steps:
Click
Show new schedule
and review it carefully.
To see a detailed version, click
Sign
, then on the next screen click
Payment schedule
to download and check the PDF.
If everything looks good, click
Sign
again and follow the on-screen instructions.
Who can change their payment schedule?
You qualify if:
If there is one loan agreement, you must have paid at least one full monthly payment within the valid payment schedule.
If you have more than one loan, you can reschedule only after you have paid at least the first monthly payment under the new agreement in full.
If you have recently changed your schedule, you can use the service again if you have paid the full amount of at least one new scheduled monthly payment.
You can change your payment schedule as long as the contract is not terminated/loan defaulted.
If you have outstanding debt/invoices- in this case, your debt will be added to the new schedule, which may increase your future monthly payments.
