TOPIC: What Is B Secure
QUESTION: What is B-Secure?
--- POLICY ANSWER ---
B-Secure is an add-on service that costs €10 per month. It allows borrowers to automatically restructure their loan terms online without manual approval. Features include changing the monthly payment date, requesting a payment holiday (pausing principal repayments), or extending the total loan period to reduce monthly bills.
